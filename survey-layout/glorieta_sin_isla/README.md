# Garage lot + 4 lots around a circle without an island

- **Circle:** 15.5 m across, no island. A straight 7 m entry, 4.06 m wide, connects it to the existing passage. Road area 217 m².
- **Lot 1:** 49 m² (7.2 m front, about 6.9 m deep), for a 2-car garage.
- **Lots 2–5:** 325.9 m² each. Frontage on the road is 9.5, 9.5, 9.8 and 23.3 m.
- **Saleable:** 1,352.6 m², 86% of the 1,569.8 m² parcel.

## U-turn test
The vehicle comes in from the passage, loops around the circle and leaves forwards. The path is idealised; dimensions are typical, assumed values.

| Vehicle | Minimum clearance |
|---|---|
| Car | 56 cm |
| Pickup | 30 cm |
| Van | 11 cm |
| Garbage or fire truck | does not fit |

The tightest point is the 4 m passage mouth, not the circle, so a bigger circle does not help. At 16.5 m the clearances are unchanged.
At 14.5 m a van has 3 cm; at 13.5 m only a car fits.
