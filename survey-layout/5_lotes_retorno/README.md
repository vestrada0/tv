# 5 lots with a hammerhead turnaround (recommended)

The parcel is 1,569.8 m², taken from the dimensions on the Ruiz de Haro drawing.

**Road:** 155.5 m² in total.
- Lane 4.06 m wide × 22 m long, continuing the existing passage to Calle Libertad.
- Hammerhead (T-shaped) turnaround at the end, 15 m across × 4.5 m deep, with arms reaching 5.5 m past each side of the lane. A car can turn around with a three-point turn.
- Pull-in bay 2.5 × 7 m just inside the entrance, so a car leaving can wait while a car arriving passes. The route from the street to the turnaround is straight, so drivers can see each other.
